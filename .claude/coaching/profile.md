# Fitness Profile

## About Me
Douglas, 31 y.o (born 95), male, skinny, 184cm tall, ~60kg, swedish. I have a light scoliosis which my physical therapist told me might be fine as long as I move my body enough. I work as a software engineer, sitting down at my computer 8h a day.

## Fitness History
Have worked out on and off in my adult life, mainly doing cardio. Sometimes I have been running regularly, and sometimes I have been doing sports including squash and muay thai. I have worked out my core for the past 5 months.

## Current Routine
For muscle training, I work out with this app about 2 times a week. I work out in the morning, about 1h after I wake up and have had a coffee. I work out for about 1h, mixing core exercises for glutes, abs, and lower back. The reason I started working out was to prevent lower back pain.

I dont do any formal exercise besides that, but I do bike to work every day which is about a 30-40 minute bike ride both ways.

## Current Performance Level
After 5 months of training, performance has increased steadily without major plateaus. Current benchmarks include 4x65s side planks and 4x35s hollow body holds. Has progressed into intermediate/advanced exercises.

## Back Pain & Scoliosis Status
The back pain and scoliosis symptoms are basically gone now. Before starting training, felt "twisted" pretty much all the time, with actual back pain episodes a few times a month. Was worried it would get worse with age, which was the primary motivation to start. After 5 months of consistent core training, the twisting sensation and pain episodes have essentially stopped.

## Goals
The main goal is to stay healthy, feel good in my body, enjoy exercise and movement, and prevent physical pain and injuries. Besides that, I find it interesting to see if I can get stronger and do new things with my body - explore what I would be able to do if I trained more.

## Constraints
- Timewise I am quite flexible, because I work remotely and can set pretty much any schedule I want. I can start and end work whenever it suits me
- I work out at home
- I have a small apt
- I work from a coworking space, where I spend most of the day, so I need to work out before I leave my home or after I get back. In theory, if I needed to work out in the middle of the day, I could stay home for that day, but I really value going to the cowork to meet people, so I prefer doing the workouts outside those hours.
- I have a low budget, which is why I do home workouts.

## Equipment
- The only equipment I currently own is a yoga mat and some elastic loop bands, but I am open to extending that if there is something super high-leverage stuff to get.

## What Motivates Me
Unclear? The feeling of having a stronger back and not feeling pain is quite motivating. I enjoy the feeling after a workout when I feel my muscles having been activated. When I was doing sports I had a lot of fun, so that sort of motivated me just because it was a social and technical activity (especially thai boxing).

## What Discourages Me
- Usually stress or scheduling conflicts, feeling like have other things that are pulling me
- Feeling tired, hungover, or sleep-deprived
- When other people are staying over in the apt and its hard to find room for the workout
- Doing the same thing over and over without results or variation
- Getting bored with the current routine

## How the Current Routine Feels
The routine is clearly working for its original purpose (back pain is gone), but there's growing boredom. Questions whether mixing all muscle groups every session is the best approach for muscle building, or if shorter daily workouts focused on one area would be better.

## Sleep
Mostly good. Averages about 7.5 hours. Occasionally wakes up early and can't get back to sleep, but this is rare now (about once a month). Was more common before.

## Injuries & Mobility
No injuries or mobility issues beyond the scoliosis (which is now managed). No lingering issues from muay thai or squash.

## Other Relevant Context
Trying to eat a lot of protein because I have heard it helps muscle building. Mostly protein bars and chicken.
