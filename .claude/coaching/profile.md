# Fitness Profile

## About Me
Douglas, 31 y.o (born 95), male, skinny, 184cm tall, ~60kg, swedish. I have a light scoliosis which my physical therapist told me might be fine as long as I move my body enough. I work as a software engineer, sitting down at my computer 8h a day.

## Fitness History
Have worked out on and off in my adult life, mainly doing cardio. Sometimes I have been running regularly, and sometimes I have been doing sports including squash and muay thai. I have worked out my core for the past 5 months.

## Current Routine
Transitioning from 2x/week full-body 60-minute sessions to daily (weekday) 20-minute focused sessions with rolling muscle group rotation (Abs → Glutes → Lower Back). Floor is 3 sessions/week. Works out in the morning, about 1h after waking up with coffee, before heading to coworking space.

Also bikes to work every day, about 30-40 minutes each way.

## Current Performance Level
After 5 months of training, performance has increased steadily without major plateaus. Current benchmarks include 4x65s side planks and 4x35s hollow body holds. Has progressed into intermediate/advanced exercises.

## Back Pain & Scoliosis Status
The back pain and scoliosis symptoms are basically gone now. Before starting training, felt "twisted" pretty much all the time, with actual back pain episodes a few times a month. Was worried it would get worse with age, which was the primary motivation to start. After 5 months of consistent core training, the twisting sensation and pain episodes have essentially stopped.

## Goals
- Stay healthy, feel good in body, enjoy exercise and movement, prevent pain and injuries
- Genuinely interested in pushing further: building more muscle, expanding to other muscle groups, exploring other exercise practices for overall health
- Wants training to feel engaging, not mechanical - looking for skill development and variety
- Considering upper body work (bodyweight) as next expansion

## Constraints
- Timewise quite flexible - works remotely, can set own schedule
- Works out at home in a small apartment
- Spends most of day at coworking space - prefers working out before leaving or after returning
- Low budget - home workouts preferred
- 20-minute morning sessions fit well into daily routine

## Equipment
- Yoga mat
- Elastic loop bands
- Considering a doorframe pull-up bar (~200-400 SEK) - needs to check if doorframes allow it

## What Motivates Me
- The feeling of a stronger back and absence of pain
- Feeling muscles activated after a workout
- Social and technical activities (enjoyed muay thai and squash for these reasons)
- Seeing progress and being able to do new things
- Curiosity about what his body could do with more training

## What Discourages Me
- Stress or scheduling conflicts
- Feeling tired, hungover, or sleep-deprived
- When other people are staying over and space is limited
- Doing the same thing over and over without results or variation
- Getting bored with the current routine
- Workouts feeling mechanical, solitary, and goalless

## How the Current Routine Feels
The routine achieved its original purpose (back pain is gone), but boredom is growing. Described it as "brushing teeth but way more cumbersome." Lacks skill development, social element, and a compelling forward goal. Switching to shorter daily focused sessions is partly to address this.

## Sleep
Mostly good. Averages about 7.5 hours. Occasionally wakes up early and can't get back to sleep, but this is rare now (about once a month).

## Injuries & Mobility
No injuries or mobility issues beyond the scoliosis (which is now managed). No lingering issues from muay thai or squash.

## Nutrition
Trying to eat a lot of protein for muscle building. Mostly protein bars and chicken.
