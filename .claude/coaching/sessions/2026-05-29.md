# Session - 2026-05-29

## First Coaching Session

### Topics Discussed

**1. Boredom with current routine**
- Douglas identified the core issue: workouts feel mechanical, solitary, goalless, and lack skill development
- Compared it to "brushing teeth but way more cumbersome" - discipline-only motivation is unsustainable
- Discussed how enjoyment is the strongest predictor of long-term exercise adherence
- The boredom isn't a character flaw - it's feedback that the routine needs to evolve

**2. Expanding beyond core-only training**
- Douglas is genuinely interested in pushing further, not just maintaining
- Discussed upper body bodyweight training (push-ups, pull-ups, rows) as a natural next step
- Discussed mobility/movement practice as a high-skill option
- Sports acknowledged as great but hard to coordinate - better as complement than foundation
- Pull-up bar discussed as high-value low-cost equipment (~200-400 SEK). Douglas will check if doorframes allow it.

**3. Daily short sessions vs. 2x/week long sessions**
- Douglas proposed 20-minute daily (weekday) sessions focused on one muscle group
- Discussed habit science: frequency builds habits faster, shorter sessions reduce resistance, total weekly volume matters more than session distribution
- Training quality is actually better with focused sessions (less fatigue accumulation)

### Key Decision: Rolling Rotation Schedule
- **Plan for 5 weekday sessions, 20 min each, one muscle group per day**
- **Rolling rotation: Abs → Glutes → Lower Back → repeat**
- **No fixed days - just "do whatever's next"**
- **Floor is 3 sessions/week, not 5** - missing days is fine, rotation self-corrects
- Advised giving himself a genuine "out" for the first two weeks - 2-3 days in week one is a success

**4. Exercise-by-exercise review**
Douglas raised specific concerns about 9 exercises. Researched each one with web sources to verify advice. Key findings:

- **Dead Bug (30 reps)**: Hip flexor fatigue confirmed as common at high reps. Recommended range is 8-12 reps. At 30, it's a hip flexor exercise, not core. Decision: retire.
- **Reverse Crunches (lower back)**: Form issue - needs to focus on "pelvic curl, not leg lift" with 3-second lowering. Decision: update instructions, retry with corrected form.
- **Hollow Body Hold (back lifting)**: Research unanimous - stop when back lifts, non-negotiable. Especially important with scoliosis history. Decision: enforce strict form, regress when needed.
- **Side Plank (second side fatigue)**: Cross-over fatigue is real but modest. McGill protocol (shorter repeated holds) is better than long grinding holds. Decision: restructure to McGill approach.
- **Mountain Climbers (blisters/mat)**: Confirmed - yoga mat is wrong surface. Socks on hard floor = sliding mountain climber, a recognized harder variation. Decision: switch to sliding variation.
- **Fire Hydrants (band blisters)**: Reposition band above knee. Decision: update notes.
- **Plank Shoulder Taps (time vs reps)**: Reps better matches the discrete movement. Decision: change to rep-based.
- **Single-Leg RDL (just feels like stretching)**: Confirmed - bodyweight version is a balance drill, not strength. Needs external load. Decision: keep but add resistance band (stand on band, hold in opposite hand).
- **Bulgarian Split Squat (feels like thighs)**: Confirmed primarily quad exercise. Can be glute-biased with technique changes, but doesn't fit core program. Decision: remove.

**5. Session structure design**
- Discussed 2 exercises x 4 sets vs 3 exercises x 3 sets. Landed on **3x3** for more within-session variety (directly addresses boredom).
- Discussed stretching: switched from full-body 8-stretch routine to **targeted 2-3 stretches** for the muscle group just worked (~2 min instead of 5 min).
- Final session structure: 3 exercises x 3 sets (~18-20 min) + targeted stretching (~2 min) = **~20-22 min total**.

### Final Session Design

| Component | Detail | Time |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Exercises | 3 exercises from one muscle group, 3 sets each | ~18-20 min |
| Stretching | 2-3 targeted stretches for that muscle group | ~2 min |
| **Total** | | **~20-22 min** |

Rotation: Abs → Glutes → Lower Back → repeat. Rolling schedule, floor of 3 sessions/week.
